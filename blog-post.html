The best crypto lending platforms

Crypto lending platforms let you borrow additional funds using your existing crypto holdings.

This allows you to increase the amount of money you have invested or available to trade with.

As good as that may sound, it means you are trusting a third-party platform to keep your money safe, while it’s being used as collateral for the loan.

So before you deposit funds anywhere, you need to carefully research the different types of crypto lending platforms available, and the risks involved.

To help you get started, we’ve highlighted 10 popular lending platforms, and reviewed key features like supported assets, blockchains, custody model, and loan-to-value ratios.

Why we chose Aave

Aave is the largest DeFi cryptocurrency lending market by total value locked (TVL). It was the first platform to popularise crypto lending, and has been pivotal to the cryptocurrency ecosystem ever since.

It is available on Ethereum and Ethereum layer-2 blockchains. Assets vary depending on the specific blockchain, but you’ll usually find ETH, USDC, USDT and a mix of other tokens, stablecoins and restaked ETH.

Users can stake AAVE to receive rewards and participate in governance.

Pros
  • +Deep liquidity. Perhaps the deepest pool in the market, due to its popularity.
  • +Low fees on layer-2. Using Aave on a layer-2 keeps transaction fees low.
  • +You keep your keys. No company holds your collateral, so there is no platform to freeze withdrawals.
Cons
  • −No real dollars. You can’t borrow real dollars, only stablecoins.
  • −Exposure beyond Aave. Events like the KelpDAO hack show how Aave is exposed to risks that occur on different platforms.
  • −Tax reporting. Lending through a DeFi platform can be difficult to reconcile at tax time – unless you use specialised software like Koinly.

What to be aware of

  • ⚠️Rates are dynamic and fluctuate based on market demand.
  • ⚠️Aave is managed by two different entities:
    • Aave Labs: The private for-profit company and core developer behind the Aave software.
    • Aave DAO: The community-run organisation managed by AAVE token holders that funds and governs the Aave platform.
  • ⚠️Aave has been battle-tested since it was created in 2017, and has never been hacked. However, assets and services linked to the platform have been compromised in the past, which shows that even the most resilient platforms still carry risk.
